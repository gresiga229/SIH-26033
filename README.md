## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is FarmLink, a digital platform that aims to connect farmers directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, hotels, and other bulk buyers. The main goal is to reduce the number of middlemen involved in the agricultural supply chain so that farmers can earn a better profit and consumers can buy products at reasonable prices.

Through this platform, farmers can upload details of their products, including quantity, quality, price, and location. Buyers can browse the available products and place orders directly with farmers.

The platform will also suggest suitable prices based on current market trends and demand. A group-buying option will allow multiple buyers to combine their orders, helping reduce transportation costs. In addition, the platform will help farmers find nearby transport services and arrange deliveries.

We also plan to use AI to predict the demand for agricultural products so that farmers can make better decisions about selling their produce and avoid unnecessary wastage.

## Technical Approach
We plan to develop FarmLink as a web and mobile-friendly platform using technologies that are suitable for building a reliable and easy-to-use application.

Technologies to be used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.
- Backend: Python and Django.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- AI and Machine Learning: Python and Scikit-learn.
- Maps and Navigation: Google Maps API.
- Payment Integration: UPI payment gateway.
Working Process:
1. Farmers and buyers register on the platform.
2. Farmers upload their products along with quantity, price and location.
3. Buyers search for the products they need and place orders directly.
4. The system suggests suitable prices by analysing market trends.
5. AI predicts the demand for different agricultural products.
6. The logistics feature helps find nearby transport providers and suitable delivery routes.
7. Buyers make payments through the platform and track their orders until delivery.

## Feasibility and Viability
Our proposed solution is feasible because it can be developed using technologies that are already available. The platform can initially be introduced in a small agricultural region and gradually expanded to other areas based on its success.

However, there may be some challenges during implementation. Some farmers may not be familiar with using digital platforms, transportation arrangements may be difficult in certain areas, and product prices may change frequently.

To overcome these challenges, we plan to provide a simple interface with regional language support. We can also work with local Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) to help farmers register and use the platform. Partnering with local transport providers can make deliveries easier and more affordable.

The platform can be maintained through a small service fee on successful transactions and optional logistics services. This will help make the platform sustainable in the long run without placing a heavy financial burden on farmers.

## Impact and Benefits
FarmLink can create positive changes for both farmers and consumers by making the agricultural supply chain more direct and transparent.

Benefits to Farmers:
- Better earnings by reducing the involvement of middlemen.
- Direct access to consumers and bulk buyers.
- Better understanding of market prices and demand.
- More opportunities to sell agricultural products.
